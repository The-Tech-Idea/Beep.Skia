MindMapLayout (class)

Radial mind-map auto-layout. The central node is placed at Center; level-1 topics are distributed around it, and deeper levels fan out within each parent's angular sector. Components not connected to the tree are placed in a row below.

MemberKindDescription
float LevelSpacingPropertyDistance from the central node to level-1 topics.
float SubLevelSpacingPropertyDistance from a parent to its child for levels deeper than one.
SKPoint CenterPropertyCenter point of the radial arrangement.
float UnconnectedSpacingPropertyVertical gap used when parking unconnected components.
void Arrange(IReadOnlyList<SkiaComponent> components, IReadOnlyList<IConnectionLine> lines)MethodGets or sets the arrange.

MindMapVisibility (class)

Applies collapse/expand visibility across a mind map: descendants of collapsed nodes (and their connecting lines) are hidden; everything else is restored to visible.

MemberKindDescription
