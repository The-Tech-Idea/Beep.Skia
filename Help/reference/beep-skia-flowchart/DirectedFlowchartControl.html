DirectedFlowchartControl class

Flowchart family — class

A flowchart shape whose flow can run left to right (inputs down the left side, outputs down the right side) or top to bottom (inputs along the top, outputs along the bottom). Each shape places its ports on its own outline in both directions, in order across the flow. Process, Decision, Start/End and the catalog shapes that draw a flow derive from it; shapes whose ports are fixed by their meaning (loops, fork, join, off-page reference, tape, connector, annotation, comment) derive from FlowchartControl and have no direction.
