DecisionNode class

Flowchart family — class

Decision node: a diamond whose ports sit on its tips. Left to right, the input is at the left tip and the outputs yes (port 0) and no (port 1) at the right and bottom tips; with ShowTopBottomPorts the flow runs top to bottom: the input at the top tip, yes at the bottom and no at the right.

string LabelPropertyGets or sets the label.
