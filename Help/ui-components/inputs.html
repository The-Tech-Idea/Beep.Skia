Input Components

Color, date and time pickers, search, switch, spinner, text area and grouped choice controls

Beyond the basic TextBox, Checkbox and Dropdown controls, Beep.Skia ships pickers, search, switches and grouped selection controls. All of them are MaterialControl components, so they participate in theming, hit testing and the property grid.
